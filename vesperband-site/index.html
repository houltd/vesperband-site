01 / About

About
the band.

Five-piece covers band based across the East Midlands.

Vesper is a five-piece rock and indie covers band based across the East Midlands. We play pubs, weddings, private parties, corporate events and festivals.

The set is around thirty songs — mostly rock and indie from the 80s and 90s, with a handful of newer tracks. Two 60-minute sets, or one longer set on request.

We supply our own PA and lighting. Bookings across the Midlands and further afield by arrangement.
