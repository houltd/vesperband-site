01 / Who We Are

High-octane
rock that fills
the room.

A working band built for venues that want a guaranteed Saturday night.

Vesper are a five-piece rock and indie covers band based in the East Midlands, drawing on three decades of stage experience between us. We play the songs people came to hear — Britpop swagger, classic rock thunder, indie singalongs, and the kind of 80s and 90s anthems that get every generation in the room moving.

Our set is built around energy. Two hours of music, thirty-plus songs, no awkward gaps, and a sound dialled in for everything from a packed pub on a Friday night to a wedding marquee to a corporate function room.

We bring our own PA and lighting, turn up early, and play loud — but we know how to read a room and pull the levels back when the speeches need to land. We're a working band, not a hobby.
